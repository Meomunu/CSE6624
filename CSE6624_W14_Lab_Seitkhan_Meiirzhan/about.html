Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the community?

Everyone who wants to connect with others, share experiences, or support someone is welcome. You don't need any special experience to join.

How can I get support?

You can choose a topic, complete our questionnaire, and tell us what kind of support you are looking for. You can also connect with people who have similar experiences.

How can I become part of the community?

Visit our Community page and join us. You can participate, listen to others, share your experience, and offer support.
