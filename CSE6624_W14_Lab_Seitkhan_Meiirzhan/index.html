You are not alone.

A cube represents different sides of life, with each face dedicated to a specific topic, such as discipline, studies, relationships, emotions, and personal growth. Each side connects you with peers who can share their experiences, offer advice, and support you when you need help. The idea is that everyone has different life experiences, and every peer can help you see a problem from another perspective.
